## 为什么 hold（必须给原因）

- **「概念稿、非真实」是社区长期共识**：r/UXDesign「Dribbble was not great for UX. Lots of half baked UI designs that didn't make sense and just looked cool but not functional」；「the majority of the designs are self invented pet projects not driven by real requirements」[1]。
- **真实性崩塌有直接证据**：r/UI_Design「Is it me or has real design examples disappeared from Dribbble?」+ AI 疲劳帖「it's always slop, better slop」[2]。
- **2026 年主流盘点已将其降级**：colorlib 定性为「visual direction and aesthetic inspiration」用途，并明确「less useful for UX patterns or technical implementation — for those, Mobbin and Page Flows are better」[3]。
- 行业迁移在发生：「Mobbin is way better for real design inspo」（2026-05 设计圈评论）[4]。

## 保留的价值

- 审美方向板（配色/排版/构图灵感）仍有量级优势；「I wish Dribbble would come back」说明社区对它有怀旧期待而非全盘否定 [1]。
- 重新入选触发器：真实项目占比回升的第三方证据。
