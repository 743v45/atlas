## 官方一手（changelog，已单独 WebFetch 全文留档）

- [Updated plans for Render workspaces | Render Changelog](https://render.com/changelog/updated-plans-for-render-workspaces)
  - Hobby（免费）替代 legacy Hobby；Pro（$25/月 flat）替代 Professional（$19/成员/月）；Scale（$499/月 flat）替代 Organization（$29/成员/月）
  - 官方口径："The new Hobby and Pro plans include less bandwidth than their legacy counterparts, because legacy plans subsidized bandwidth usage with seat fees."
  - 超额计费改按 GB："Bandwidth overages are now billed per GB instead of in 100 GB increments."，$0.15/GB；构建分钟 $5/千分；额外域名 $0.25/月
  - 过渡：2026-04-23 起 new workspaces 用新计划；存量可自愿切换至 2026-08-01；到期未动自动迁移
- [Better pricing for fast-growing teams | Render Blog](https://render.com/blog/better-pricing-for-fast-growing-teams)：取消席位费、Pro $25 flat、明确 compute 价格不变
